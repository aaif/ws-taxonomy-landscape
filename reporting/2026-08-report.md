# [Report] WS Taxonomy & Landscape – August 2026

**Working Group:** WS Taxonomy & Landscape

**Reporting period:** August 2026

**Chair(s):** Junjie Bu (Google) & Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg)

**Date submitted:** 2026-09-01

## Summary

August moved the workstream from initial term admission into definition and broader domain coverage. Across three terminology-review syncs on 10, 17 and 24 August, we reviewed 53 terms: 37 were kept, 15 were culled and one remained in debate. We closed the month with a pull request review session on 31 August, where we discussed definitions and left review comments on four pull requests.

## Progress Against Objectives

- The 3 August sync was included in the July report and is excluded from the review totals in this report.
- We held three terminology-review syncs on 10, 17 and 24 August and reviewed 53 terms. The group kept 37, culled 15 and retained `Multi-agent misdirection` for further debate.
- The 10 August sync covered Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment, Security & Privacy, and Workflows & Process Integration. We kept 9 terms, culled 6 and held 1 for further debate.
- The 17 August sync covered Observability & Traceability and Accuracy & Reliability. We kept 11 terms and culled 5.
- The 24 August sync covered core universal terms and the remaining Accuracy & Reliability candidates. We kept 17 terms and culled 4, and we resolved `Agent` as a kept universal term.
- We moved definition discussions into pull requests for 19 unique accepted terms. The `Harness` definition merged in [#37](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/37). Pull requests [#39](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/39), [#40](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/40), [#41](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/41), [#42](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/42), [#48](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/48) and [#49](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/49) remained in review at month-end.
- Definition proposals now record explicit relationships among adjacent concepts, including `Skill` and `Tool`, `Kill switch` and `Reversibility`, `Harness` and `Meta-harness`, and `Delegation` and `Handoff`.
- On 31 August, we held a pull request review session covering `Human in the loop` ([#40](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/40)), `Meta-harness` ([#48](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/48)), the Identity & Trust definitions ([#42](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/42)), and the initial Workflow & Process Integration term set ([#55](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/55)). We discussed the definitions and recorded comments on the pull requests. No pull requests merged during the session.
- Contributor proposals expanded the review pipeline for both parts of the workstream, including AVE in the landscape ([#38](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/38)), an Attestation & Verifiable Evidence landscape category ([#44](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/44)), and an initial Workflow & Process Integration term set ([#55](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/55)).

## Blockers and Risks

- Definition review throughput is the main risk to v1.0. Six definition pull requests remained open at month-end, including overlapping proposals for `Attestation`, and agreed terms still require Domain Editor review and cross-term reconciliation before they can merge.
- Landscape proposals require cross-workgroup review to confirm category boundaries and vendor-neutral inclusion before they can merge.
