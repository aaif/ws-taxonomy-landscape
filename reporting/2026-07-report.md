# [Report] WS Taxonomy & Landscape – July 2026

**Working Group:** WS Taxonomy & Landscape

**Reporting period:** July 2026

**Chair(s):** Junjie Bu (Google) & Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg)

**Date submitted:** 2026-08-04

## Summary

July was the month we moved from collecting terms to arbitrating them. Four weekly syncs took 46 terms through sequential review and admitted 13, which shipped as the workstream's first taxonomy pull request and put entries on the public portal for the first time. Counting the 3 August sync, the group has now reviewed 67 of the 128-term backlog.

## Progress Against Objectives

- We held four weekly syncs (6, 13, 20 and 27 July) and extended the meeting from 30 minutes to a full hour, to allow more time for discussion.
- We reviewed 46 terms as a group, working sequentially from the universal terms into Identity & Trust. 13 were admitted, 11 were culled, and 21 were held for further debate.
- All 13 admitted terms shipped as the workstream's first taxonomy pull request ([#33](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/33), merged 3 August): 8 universal, 4 from Identity & Trust, and 1 from Workflows & Process Integration.
- Since the period closed, the 3 August sync reviewed a further 21 terms, mostly from Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment. 8 were admitted and 4 culled, and they are open as [#35](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/35), taking the taxonomy from 13 entries to 21.
- We deferred the `definition`, `category` and `broaderTerm` fields on this pass so terms are admitted first and defined second.
- Six pull requests and three issues arrived from contributors outside the workstream, mostly against the landscape. We are holding them until the contributors join a sync, which keeps the review bar consistent.

## Blockers and Risks

- The debate backlog is the real risk to v1.0. 25 of the 67 terms reviewed are held unresolved, `Agent` among them, and these are the terms where working groups hold genuinely different positions. The first pass through the backlog will finish; the contested tail is what determines when v1.0 lands.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

None.

## Next Month's Focus

1. We will agree definitions for an initial 5 terms per working group, plus 5 to 10 universal terms, by the end of August.
2. We will move definition discussions out of the spreadsheet and into pull requests, so the debate history lives alongside the term.
3. We will publish the definitions style guide and an ownership model.
