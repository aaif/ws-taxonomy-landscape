# [Report] WS Taxonomy & Landscape – September 2026

**Working Group:** WS Taxonomy & Landscape

**Reporting period:** September 2026

**Chair(s):** Junjie Bu (Google) & Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg)

**Date submitted:** 2026-09-30

## Summary

September was the month definitions started landing. Seven definitions merged across four pull requests, taking the taxonomy from 1 to 8 defined terms, and a further 25 definitions covering three working groups and the universal terms are in open pull requests, 23 of them marked Ready for Review. The workstream also agreed to merge asynchronously once two Domain Editors approve, paused Landscape to focus on the taxonomy, and reviewed all 12 Agentic Commerce candidates, keeping 8.

## Progress Against Objectives

- Seven definitions merged: `Human curated` ([#39](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/39)), `Meta-harness` ([#48](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/48)), `Human in the loop` ([#40](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/40)), and `Trust`, `Identifier`, `Attestation` and `Delegation` ([#42](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/42)). Three of the four pull requests named for the 14 September vote have merged, and the Workflows & Process Integration term set ([#55](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/55)) is still addressing review comments.
- On 14 September, the workstream agreed on a merge rule. A definition pull request can merge asynchronously once two Domain Editors approve it and its review comments have been answered, and live votes are reserved for contested terms.
- A [Ready for Review milestone](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/milestone/1) now marks the pull requests that have been through the workstream process, which keeps review effort on the terms the group has already agreed to take forward.
- On 21 September, we reviewed all 12 Agentic Commerce candidates after Leonardo Galesky socialized them with the Agentic Commerce Working Group. We kept 8 and culled 4.
- 25 more definitions are in open pull requests. The Ready for Review milestone holds 23 of them: Observability & Traceability ([#59](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/59), 7 definitions with two approvals), Workflows & Process Integration ([#55](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/55), 7), Security & Privacy ([#56](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/56), 5) and a batch of universal terms ([#41](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/41), 4, held for cross-working-group review). `AI slop` ([#62](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/62), still a draft) and `Subagent` ([#68](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/68)) make up the remaining two. The 8 kept Agentic Commerce terms are open as [#60](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/60), with definitions to follow.
- We were asked in August who the Domain Editors are. They are our working group delegates, the representatives each working group has nominated to this workstream since it began. Each Domain Editor brings their group's candidate terms to the workstream, drafts definitions with their group, and reviews definitions. The working group that proposes a term owns its definition, and a definition merges once two Domain Editors approve it.
- On 14 September, the workstream agreed to pause Landscape work and accept taxonomy contributions only. [#61](https://github.com/aaif/ws-taxonomy-landscape/pull/61) updated the contribution guide to match. Landscape needs agreed inclusion criteria and a level of granularity before it can take contributions, and the group judged that the taxonomy needs its full attention first.

## Blockers and Risks

- **Unsolicited contributions are taking reviewer time.** Since the repository opened to the public, we have received a steady flow of pull requests and issues from people who have not joined a sync or introduced themselves, and several appear AI-generated. We decided to focus review on pull requests that have been through the workstream process, and we have asked admins for a Domain Editors GitHub team so we can require their review.

## Decisions Needed from the TC

- Please acknowledge that the workstream has deferred the Landscape deliverable in its charter to focus on the taxonomy. When we reopen it, we will bring proposed inclusion criteria to the TC first, because deciding which projects and vendors make the cut is a neutrality question for the foundation.

## Next Month's Focus

1. We will keep moving definitions through review and into the taxonomy.
2. We will start working through the debate backlog and triage the newest candidate terms.
